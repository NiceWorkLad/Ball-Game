3. Completed Work
------------------
- Basic Ball Game working and tracs score one time
- High score saved in a cookie (ballgame_highscore, kept 1 year), shown as "BEST n" under the board
- Touch screen support (pointer events: mouse, touch, multi-touch) and mobile layout
- Custom cursor: a single dot that sits exactly on the mouse (no trailing ring, so no lag), with an instant halo when clicking; mouse devices only
- Fixed game speed inconsistencies: physics now runs in fixed 4ms steps on any frame rate, drawn smoothly between steps
- Sideways kick randomness grows with score: none up to 10, ramping to the original 1-or-2 multiplier at 50 (RANDOM_START / RANDOM_FULL in script.js)
- Arrow at the top edge follows the ball when it is above the screen (shrinks/fades with height)
- Forgiving touch: bigger hit area for fingers (1.7x ball radius vs 1.05x mouse), taps count if the ball arrives within 120ms after the finger lifts or was there within the last 80ms, fast swipes count. Tunable via HIT_SCALE / TAP_BUFFER_MS / LATE_TAP_MS in script.js
- Page zoom disabled (pinch, double-tap, iOS gestures) so two-thumb play can't zoom the page; auto-resets if it ever gets zoomed
- Settings screen (same box as the board): sound on/off + volume and ball size, saved in cookies. The old theme and font choices were removed when the art style was finalised (their cookies are ignored)
- Settings open from a gear icon in the top-right corner of the board (start and game over screens). The corner sound button was removed; sound lives in settings
- Icons are Lucide (open source, ISC licence) embedded as an SVG sprite in index.html; originals + licence in icons/
- Settings menu scrolls inside the box (finger, mouse wheel, keyboard) with the Done row pinned at the top and a fade hint while there is more below. Only the board blocks all touch gestures; the rest of the page allows panning but not zooming
- Ball sizes: Normal (original, radius 60), Big (80), Huge (100), each with its own best score. Normal keeps the original ballgame_highscore cookie; others use ballgame_highscore_big / _huge. Touch grace margin and sideways kick are scaled so bigger balls play the same
- Art style (final, the only option): minimalist, low-poly, colourful gradients, always gently moving ("moving art"). The whole board is drawn on the canvas as flat-shaded triangles lit from the top left ("Art" section of script.js): gradient sky, drifting parallax scenery layers, a faceted white ball that spins with its sideways speed. Each stage has its own palette (PALETTES in script.js): bright morning, golden sunset, pink/violet dusk, deep night. The page behind the board is a gradient in the same colours (--g1..--g3, --accent) that eases between stages via CSS @property. Font: Outfit. Start / game over / settings screens are tinted glass panels
- Performance note: CSS backdrop blur and animated page backgrounds were tried and dropped; they cost a lot of frame rate (a blur over a board that redraws every frame). Motion lives on the canvas, which draws in well under 1ms
- Hit sounds (Web Audio, random clip of the 8 per hit, unlocked on first tap) with a mute button (saved in a cookie)
- Start, game over and paused screens. Dropping the ball after the first hit ends the game; tap to play again
- Auto-pause when the tab/app goes to the background
- The Climb to 1000 (replaces the old 4 stages, branch climb-to-1000). Every hit after the serve is one step up a 1000-hit climb from the pavement to the Moon; when the ball drops the go ends but the height is kept. The record is reaching the Moon in the fewest goes. The world is one tall strip of 8 sections, 125 hits each (STAGES in script.js): Pavement, Shops & market, Apartments, Skyscrapers, Birds & planes, Space station, Space void, The Moon. A camera follows the climb (about 1/80 of a board per hit, eased) so the world creeps down past the board. Section art is placeholder for now (sky gradient, faceted shapes, a marker line per section); see the concept plan for build steps 2-8
- Climb saved per ball size: cookie ballgame_climb_<size> = "height:goes"; fewest goes to the Moon in ballgame_moon_goes_<size>. Furthest section in ballgame_section_<size> (the old ballgame_stage_* cookies are ignored)
- Landing screen at 1000: goes taken, medal (Gold <= 20, Silver <= 40, Bronze <= 60; MEDALS in script.js), new record. The next tap starts a fresh climb from the pavement
- Settings > Climb: progress, record, and "New climb" (asks first; only finished climbs count towards the record)
- Journey track on the start and drop screens: a dot per section, the current one named and the next one's starting height. Hit sounds get slightly higher each section
